# Kennwerk — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Evidence lead at an expert commission with an annual reporting duty

- As an evidence lead, I want each indicator tagged to a building block and typed leading or lagging with its expected lag, so that my report does not judge a two-year-old strategy on publication counts that cannot move for six years.
- As an evidence lead, I want the report to list building blocks that have no leading indicator, so that the gap in our own measurement base is a published finding rather than something reviewers discover for us.
- As an evidence lead, I want to freeze a methods release before publication and issue any later change as a named restatement, so that our figures can be cited without the risk that they quietly change underneath the citation.
- As an evidence lead, I want the number of attributed versus merely associated findings reported in the summary, so that we never imply our indicators prove a policy worked.

### Research-policy analyst

- As an analyst, I want to record an attribution share for a general technology programme with the basis I used and send it for second review, so that the reconstructed research-spend figure is an auditable artefact rather than my private arithmetic.
- As an analyst, I want a change to an attribution basis to recompute every dependent figure and open a restatement, so that correcting one programme does not leave three published totals inconsistent.
- As an analyst, I want to convert a spend estimate into headcount using a recorded cost assumption, so that the assumption is visible and can be updated across the series when salaries move.
- As an analyst, I want to attempt to publish a ranking against another country on an indicator whose definitions do not align and have the system refuse it and offer the indicative-only form instead, so that a comparability problem is caught before it becomes a headline.
- As an analyst, I want to price a capability gap against a named comparator institute's known funding level, so that a recommendation to close it carries a number rather than an adjective.

### Ministry research-monitoring officer

- As a monitoring officer, I want to issue an evidence pack in answer to a parliamentary inquiry with the observations, method version and confidence grade attached, so that the answer is reproducible when the same question returns in the next session.
- As a monitoring officer, I want to see which of our figures rest on reconstruction rather than measurement, so that I know which answers will not survive a follow-up question and which programmes need proper labelling at source.
- As a monitoring officer, I want the compute and hardware dependency indicators reported alongside capability, so that a supply or export-control shock can be discussed with a baseline rather than improvised.

### Federal-state science ministry analyst

- As a state analyst, I want to submit our faculty capacity and school curriculum coverage figures against the national definition and have the published national figure state which states are covered, estimated or missing, so that our contribution is counted correctly rather than estimated for us, and uneven coverage is not presented as a complete national picture.

### Methods reviewer and administrator

- As a methods reviewer, I want to reproduce a published figure from its recorded derivation method, inputs and method version, so that an external challenge can be answered with a reconstruction rather than a defence.
- As an administrator, I want disclosure suppression enforced below a minimum cell size on talent and firm-level series, so that a confidential data supply agreement cannot be breached by an analyst assembling an otherwise reasonable table.
- As an administrator, I want indicator definitions versioned with an effective date and a stated effect on the series, so that redefining what counts as a company with strong AI competencies produces a restatement rather than an unexplained jump.
